# FileCraft: SEO + AdSense launch checklist

**No website code can guarantee immediate AdSense approval or Google rankings.** The operator is responsible for complete public content, eligibility, privacy and consent compliance.

## Website preparation

- Serve a stable canonical HTTPS domain using `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL=https://yourdomain.com`.
- Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_INDEXABLE=true` after deploying actual content and checking all public links.
- Verify `robots.txt` allows public crawling and `/sitemap.xml` lists current, genuinely available tool pages.
- Keep `/login`, `/register`, `/dashboard`, `/admin`, and `/api` out of search indexing.
- Publish original titles/descriptions, accurate tool instructions and honest conversion limitations.
- Replace every example and generic legal statement with business-specific, locally appropriate privacy/terms/contact information: company/person operating the service, contact email, how accounts, cookies, ads and MySQL records are used, retention/deletion, third-party processors and data-rights contact.
- Ensure pages are not thin/duplicated solely to attract ad traffic.
- Test mobile, accessibility, page load and conversion success with actual sample files.

## Google Search Console

1. Verify domain or URL-prefix property.
2. Add `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` from your own Search Console property to server environment.
3. Rebuild, inspect page HTML meta verification and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
4. Review indexing and crawl errors over time, not one day.

## AdSense

1. An eligible account holder must meet Google's age requirement (18+, or parent/guardian account where appropriate).
2. Apply only after the site is publicly accessible and content and legal pages are complete.
3. In AdSense add your real domain. Configure `NEXT_PUBLIC_ADSENSE_CLIENT=ca-pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX` **using your real assigned publisher ID**, not a placeholder.
4. Rebuild and check that the AdSense verification script is present and `/ads.txt` includes `google.com, pub-XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0`.
5. Complete any additional identity, address, bank and payments checks required by Google.
6. In EEA, UK and Switzerland, use a **Google-certified CMP with IAB TCF** as required for ads. The package does NOT include such a CMP, and does not claim one is installed.
7. Use clear ad placements that do not mimic download/process buttons. Do not encourage accidental clicks.
8. Wait for Google's review and correct feedback. Approval is never immediate or guaranteed.

No live ads or earnings are included in the source package. An AdSense publisher ID cannot be generated by the website.
